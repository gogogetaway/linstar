What We Supply

Wood panel supply organized around buyer applications.

Our product range is arranged by how buyers source: concrete formwork panels for construction, furniture panels for manufacturing, and engineered wood products for project supply. Each inquiry is matched by application, size, thickness, surface, core, packing and destination market.

Formwork Panels Film faced plywood, PP plastic faced plywood, slip-resistant plywood and formply. Furniture Panels Commercial plywood, MDF, chipboard and melamine boards for furniture production. Engineered Wood LVL, H20 beams and formwork components for industrial and construction supply.

Factory & Production

From veneer preparation to export packing.

Production follows controlled steps covering veneer preparation, layup, pressing, sanding, trimming, surface lamination, inspection, edge sealing and shipment packing.

01 Log preparationLogs are sorted and peeled into veneer sheets.
02 Veneer dryingVeneer moisture is stabilized before layup.
03 Gluing & layupCore, glue and structure are matched to order use.
04 Cold pressPanel stacks are pre-pressed before hot pressing.
05 Hot pressHeat and pressure cure the glue line.
06 SandingPanels are calibrated for thickness and surface.
07 TrimmingSheets are squared to the required dimensions.
08 Surface laminationFilm or overlay surfaces are applied when required.
09 QC inspectionFace, bonding, moisture and dimensions are checked.
10 Edge sealingEdges are sealed for formwork and shipment needs.
11 Export packingPallets, labels and wrapping are prepared.
12 Container loadingLoading photos and shipment documents can be arranged.

Specification control before shipment.

For repeat orders, stable specification is more important than a long promise list. We focus on the items buyers check most often before payment and container loading.
