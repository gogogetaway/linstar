Company Structure

One company group. One export division. Two production bases.

Under the same company group, JIALINDA is responsible for overseas sales, customer communication, order follow-up, export documents and shipment coordination, while Huaxin and Yongxiang support manufacturing, quality control and product development.

Why this matters to buyers

Buyers work with one responsible export team while the order is connected directly with factory production, QC, packing, documents and container loading. This reduces sourcing uncertainty and makes repeat orders easier to manage.

Integrated Manufacturing System

JIALINDA connects export service with our production foundation.

Our manufacturing group has more than 26 years of experience in the plywood and wood panel industry. The group operates two production bases: Pizhou Huaxin Wood Industry, established in 2000, and Xuzhou Yongxiang Wood Industry, established in 2011.
