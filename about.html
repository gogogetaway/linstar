Who We Are

JIALINDA is the export coordination window.

JIALINDA is the dedicated foreign trade export window, responsible for customer communication, order follow-up, packing requirements, documentation and container loading coordination.

Formwork Panels Film faced plywood, PP plastic faced plywood, slip-resistant plywood and formply. Furniture Panels Commercial plywood, MDF, chipboard and melamine boards for furniture production. Engineered Wood LVL, H20 beams and formwork components for industrial and construction supply.
