Two factories for a broader product range.

Our two factories focus on complementary panel categories, supported by veneer peeling, hot pressing, sanding, edge sealing and PP coating equipment.

Factory 1

Focus: PP plastic faced plywood, LVL, H20 timber beams and furniture panels, supported by in-house quality testing.

Factory 2
