QC & Order Control

Technical QC and order control for stable panel quality.

From sample confirmation to bulk production, JIALINDA controls key panel quality points including moisture content, bonding strength, thickness tolerance, core quality and surface grade.

In-house QC Testing Room

Our production bases are equipped with an in-house QC testing room for routine panel checks.

Routine checks are performed before or during shipment preparation, helping buyers reduce risks such as unstable moisture, thickness variation, weak bonding, core defects and inconsistent surface grade.

Routine test items

What the internal QC room can check before shipment.

Moisture content Bonding strength Thickness tolerance Size accuracy Surface grade Core quality Formaldehyde emission screening
Moisture & Thickness Control Moisture content is tested to ensure panels remain stable during sea transport and storage. Thickness tolerance is checked against order specification to avoid site-fitting issues.
Bonding & Delamination Check Glue line quality and bonding strength are tested to prevent delamination risk, especially for formwork panels exposed to water and repeated concrete pours.
Core & Surface Grade Inspection Panels are inspected for void cores, overlapping veneers, core gaps, face veneer grade and film lamination quality before packing.
Third-party Testing When Required Routine checks are performed in-house. Third-party laboratory testing can be arranged when certified reports or buyer-specified standards are required.
