# Prospect Research: Latin America

Checked: 2026-09-02

## Selected companies

1. Trade Forest Peru: direct importer of China-origin panels, imported plywood,
   double-film phenolic plywood and OSB.
   Source: https://www.tradeforestperu.com/
2. CASSAFORMA Proyectos: Peruvian formwork specialist selling 18 mm phenolic
   panels, H20 beams, props and scaffolding.
   Source: https://cassaformaproyectos.com/
3. Naturex Company: stocks film-faced phenolic plywood, pine plywood,
   finger-joint panels and HDF door skins.
   Source: https://naturexcompany.com/productos/
4. Hepta Chile: direct importer and closed-pallet wholesaler of phenolic
   formwork panels, with a main 18 mm / 120 gsm offer.
   Source: https://heptachile.cl/categoria-producto/terciados/fenolicos/
5. Los Teques: Costa Rican supplier of 12, 15 and 18 mm phenolic plywood for
   concrete formwork.
   Source: https://tequescr.com/tableros-de-contrachapado
6. MB-Triplay: direct Mexican importer of plywood, MDF, melamine and formwork
   panels, including products sourced from China.
   Source: https://www.mbtriplay.mx/
7. Triplay Maya: direct Mexican importer sourcing wood products from Asia and
   other regions.
   Source: https://www.triplaymaya.com.mx/
8. Grupo San Juan: Mexican distributor of film-faced phenolic plywood, MDO,
   form plywood and MDF.
   Source: https://www.triplaymaderasjr.com.mx/
