## Qualification notes

- Safari Group: major Australian formply importer. Its structural range requires
  AS 6669 and AS 2269 compliance, while it also carries non-structural formply.
  Source: https://www.safarigroup.com.au/formwork/formply
- Formply Australia: specialist wholesaler of F17 Class 2 structural Formply and
  LVL. Do not approach with structural claims until product-specific Australian
  certification and FSC scope are verified.
  Source: https://formplyaustralia.com.au/about/
- Nassguard Trading: lists 12, 15 and 18 mm film-faced plywood for formwork and
  supplies multiple Saudi regions.
  Source: https://nassguard.sa/product/film-faced-plywood-in-jeddah/
- Yatco Gulf: advertises bulk film-faced plywood supply to contractors and
  developers in Saudi Arabia.
  Source: https://www.yatcogulf.com/film-faced-plywood-supplier-in-saudi-arabia.php
- Saplings Timber Trading: wholesale importer that explicitly sells Chinese and
  Brazilian film-face plywood in 18 and 21 mm.
  Source: https://www.saplings.co.za/boards-film-face
- Marine Boards Kenya: engineered-panel supplier covering plywood, marine board,
  OSB, blockboard, MDF and particleboard.
  Source: https://marineboards.co.ke/contact.html
- Kamowood Limited: stocks multiple 1220 x 2440 x 18 mm marine plywood product
  lines in Ghana.
  Source: https://kamowoodltd.com/marine-plywood/
- Feranliz Wood Limited: sells trade quantities of H20 beams, marine boards and
  formwork equipment in Nigeria.
  Source: https://feranlizwood.com/building-materials/

## Outreach guardrails

- Do not claim Australian structural compliance unless the exact offered SKU and
  certificate scope have been checked.
- Do not promise a fixed number of plywood reuses. Ask for the buyer's target and
  quote against the confirmed construction and handling conditions.
- Keep certification language product-specific and subject to the final quotation.
- Use an online catalog link in the first email instead of a large attachment.
