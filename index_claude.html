Before You Inquire

Common buyer questions

What should I send to get an accurate quote?+

Product type, size, thickness, surface/film, core, glue grade, quantity and destination port. The more detail you share, the faster and more precise our quotation.

Can you mix several products in one container?+

Yes. Film faced plywood, furniture panels, MDF, LVL and H20 beams can be combined in one container when dimensions and loading weight allow.

Do you support OEM packing and private marks?+

Yes — private marks, pallet labels, wrapping, edge color and packing photos are prepared to your requirements.

What is your MOQ and lead time?+

Typical MOQ is one 20ft/40HQ container; mixed loads are possible. Lead time is usually 15–25 days depending on specification and current stock.

Which certificates and documents can you provide?+

ISO 9001, FSC, CE/EN 13986, EPA TSCA VI and KS, plus commercial invoice, packing list, loading photos and market-related certificate files checked before shipment.
