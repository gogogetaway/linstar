Film faced plywood

Brown/Red/Black Film Faced Plywood

High-performance formwork panel for concrete casting projects, widely used in floors, walls, bridges and repeated site applications.

Получить предложение

Обзор компании

Built for repeat concrete formwork.

Film faced plywood uses a coated surface over selected plywood core structures. It is designed to reduce water absorption, support clean concrete release and improve repeat-use performance when handled and maintained properly.
