What buyers care about first

Stable specifications before a lower quote.

For plywood and building material orders, price only makes sense after the specification is clear. We help confirm the structure that affects use, cost and repeat orders.

  • Core material, glue type, face grade and thickness tolerance
  • Surface requirements for formwork, furniture or decorative use
  • Packing method, pallet marks and container loading plan

Built for remote purchasing

Visibility helps buyers place repeat orders with confidence.

Most overseas buyers cannot stand beside the production line. The page should tell them what they can expect before shipment, not just say we are professional.

  • Order details checked against confirmed quotation
  • Photos for packing, marks and loading when required
