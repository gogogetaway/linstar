Factory Supply

Organized production and ready-to-ship panel inventory.

From veneer preparation to shipment-ready panels, our workflow helps buyers understand how repeat plywood orders are organized before loading and export execution.

Production workflow

12 visible production steps from veneer to shipment-ready panels.

The workflow shows how plywood orders move through production before quality checks, packing and export coordination.

01 Log selection & peeling

Logs are sorted, prepared and peeled into veneer sheets.

02 Veneer drying

Veneer moisture is stabilized before panel assembly.

03 Gluing & lay-up

Core veneers are glued and assembled in cross-grain layers.

04 Cold press

Panel stacks are pre-pressed before hot pressing.

05 Hot press

Heat and pressure cure the glue line.

06 Surfacing putty

Surface defects are repaired before sanding and finishing.

07 Sanding

Panels are calibrated for thickness and surface.

08 Trimming

Sheets are squared to the required dimensions.

09 Surface lamination

Film, veneer or decorative surfaces are applied by order requirement.

10 Routine inspection

Panels are checked for surface, bonding and dimensions.

11 Edge sealing

Edges are sealed or finished according to product use and packing needs.

12 Packing & shipment handoff

Finished panels are packed and prepared for loading coordination.
