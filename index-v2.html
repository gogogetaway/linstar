See how we make it

Every panel moves through 12 controlled factory steps, from log preparation to packed shipment.

See our full 12-step process →

Log selection & peeling

Logs are sorted, prepared and peeled into veneer sheets.

Veneer drying & storage

Veneers are dried, stacked and stabilized before layup.

Gluing & lay-up

Core veneers are glued and assembled in cross-grain layers.

Cold press

Panels are consolidated before moving into hot pressing.

Hot press

Heat and pressure cure the glue line into a rigid panel.

Surfacing with putty

Face defects are filled before sanding and finishing.

Sanding

Panels are calibrated and sanded for the target surface.

Trimming

Finished sheets are squared and cut to final dimensions.

Surface lamination

Film or overlay surfaces are pressed onto selected panels.

QC inspection

Panels are checked for face, bond and dimensional quality.

Edge sealing

Edges are sealed for moisture resistance and traceability.

Packaging & shipping

Approved panels are packed, marked and loaded for export.
