Buyer questions

Answer the questions before the buyer emails.

What should I provide for a quotation?

Please send product type, application, size, thickness, surface, quantity, destination port and required certificates.

Can BLXING support OEM packing?

Yes, the quotation can include customer marks, pallet labels, wrapping requirements and shipping documentation.

Which products should I start with?

Construction buyers usually start with film faced plywood or formply. Furniture buyers usually start with MDF, chipboard or melamine boards.
