Common buyer questions

Practical answers for plywood importers before sending a first inquiry.

Get a Quote →

Can you mix products in one container?

Yes. Film faced plywood, furniture panels, LVL and H20 beams can be arranged in one sourcing plan when dimensions and loading weight allow.

Do you support OEM packing?

Private marks, pallet labels, wrapping, edge color and packing photos can be prepared according to buyer requirements.

What documents can be prepared?

Commercial invoice, packing list, loading photos and market-related certificate files can be checked before shipment.

What should I send for a quote?

Send product type, size, thickness, surface, core, glue, quantity and destination port for faster specification matching.
