The B2B Order Security & Flow

How we coordinate the details to ensure stable panel quality and hassle-free customs clearance.

01

Specification Matching

We confirm exact technical details: core wood species (poplar/eucalyptus/birch), glue line grade (WBP/Melamine), thickness tolerances, and destination market standards.

02

OEM & Brand Marking

Application of custom edge-sealing painting (moisture protection), logo film printing, client-specific pallet tagging, and export strapping.

03

Mixed Loading Plans

Optimize container volume by mixing products (e.g. film faced plywood + LVL planks + H20 beams) in one sourcing plan, reducing total shipping and handling costs.

04

Compliance & Traceability

Provision of complete export records, certificates of origin, FSC-certified supply logs, and EUDR (European Union Deforestation Regulation) compliance details.

Technical Compliance & Quality Checks

Professional buyers require compliance. Our manufacturing bases carry out standardized testing before loading to verify performance and dimensions.

CE / EN 13986 FSC CoC ISO 9001:2015 CARB / EPA VI EUDR Compliant
✓

Bonding & Glue Strength

Standardized water-boiling tests (up to 72 hours for WBP phenolic glue) to ensure panels do not delaminate under outdoor concrete conditions.

✓

Dimensional Tolerances

Precision checking of panel thickness, diagonal correctness, and edge straightness to match strict building contractor guidelines.

✓

Moisture Content Monitoring

Verifying moisture levels (8% - 12%) before packaging to prevent warpage, mold, or structural degradation during sea transport.
