Product Specifications


Parameter

Details

Thickness

8mm up to 25mm (Customizable)

Size

1220mm x 2440mm (Standard) or Custom Sizes

Surface Material

Brown/Black/Red Film

Glue

Key Advantages

✅Enhanced Efficiency

Ultra-smooth surface ensures high-quality concrete finishes, eliminating the need for plastering and reducing labor/time costs by 30%.

Lightweight design (50% lighter than steel templates), easy to handle, and speeds up installation.

✅Superior Durability

Film coating prevents water absorption, mold growth, and deformation, extending service life.

Withstands extreme temperatures (-20°C to 60°C) and harsh chemical exposure.

✅ Cost-Effective & Eco-Friendly

Reusability lowers total costs by 40% compared to standard templates.

E1-grade eco-certified materials, recyclable and reducing construction waste.

✅ Customization Flexibility

Supports tailored solutions (pre-cut holes, special shapes) for complex structural requirements.
