PRODUCT DETAIL

 Specifications 


Parameter

Details

Core Material

Particleboard (PB) or Medium-Density Fiberboard (MDF). or Plywood. Optional moisture-resistant cores for damp environments.

Surface Finish

Coated with melamine resin-impregnated decorative paper for a hard, smooth surface.

Available in woodgrain/solid colors/metallic finishes.

Thickness

6mm (1/4") to 25mm (1"), with 16mm and 18mm most popular for furniture.

Standard sheets

1220mm × 2440mm (4' × 8'), customizable sizes for specific projects

Edge Options:

Pre-edged with ABS/PVC trim, postforming edges, or raw edges for custom finishing.

Moisture Resistance

MR (Moisture-Resistant), BWR (Boiling Water Resistant), or BWP (Boiling Water Proof).

Fire & Moisture Ratings:

Optional fire-retardant (FR) or moisture-resistant (MR) treatments.(waterproof).

Certifications

CARB Phase2,E1/E0, and GREENGUARD standards.



Advantages 

✅Durable & Scratch-Resistant:
Hard melamine surface resists scratches, stains, and daily wear, ideal for high-traffic areas.

✅Low Maintenance:
Easy to clean with a damp cloth; no need for polishing or sealing.

✅Aesthetic Versatility:
Mimics wood, stone, or abstract designs at a fraction of the cost of natural materials.

✅Moisture & Heat Resistance:
Suitable for kitchens, bathrooms, and commercial spaces (with MR core).

✅Cost-Effective:
Budget-friendly alternative to solid wood or laminates without compromising aesthetics.

✅Easy Installation:
Lightweight and compatible with standard cutting/joining tools (saws, drills, screws).

 

Applications

✅Furniture Manufacturing:

Cabinets, wardrobes, shelves, office desks, and modular furniture.

✅Interior Renovation:

Wall paneling, decorative doors, countertops, and ceiling cladding.

✅Retail & Hospitality:

Shop fixtures, display units, hotel furniture, and partition walls.

✅DIY Projects:

Custom storage solutions, craft tables, and home decor items.

✅Commercial Spaces:

Laboratory surfaces, school furniture, and healthcare facilities (with antimicrobial options).

 

Additional Tips

✅Pair with Quality Edging: Use heat-resistant or waterproof edge bands for seamless durability.

✅Eco-Friendly Choice: Opt for boards with recycled cores or formaldehyde-free adhesives.
