Regular maintenance is good for furniture
2024-12-26
Believe that buying a custom furniture consumers with furniture maintenance, durable and light of custom furniture without daily maintenance..

Believe that buying a custom furniture consumers with furniture maintenance, durable and light of custom furniture without daily maintenance, commonly used furniture maintain article, wax and maintenance agent, wax is used for wood, the material such as polyester, paint, fire rubber sheet furniture, with faint scent, maintains agent is suitable for all kinds of wood, glass, synthetic material such as wood or beauty resistant plate furniture, wax regularly to ash, etc.

When there is dust on the surface of furniture, should use wool duster or soft cloth to remove dust, if there is dirt on the surface, should use sand wax to wipe off. Hardware component is wiped with dry soft cloth, do not use the cleaner of chemical substance or acid liquid clean, avoid to damage or scrape the surface of the wardrobe as far as possible, if the surface has slight scratch and bruise, can dip in with cloth soot and lemon juice mixture besmear brush, wax after drying can. Check the furniture connector regularly, find the loose fastening in time, and inject a small amount of lubricating oil.

When clean usable half wet dishcloth wipes cabinet body, cabinet door, avoid by all means use caustic cleaner, the dust of the track USES cleaner or small bristle brush to clean can, ark frame, pull rod wait for metal part to wipe with dry cloth.
